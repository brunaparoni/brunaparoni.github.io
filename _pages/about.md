Bruna is a **PhD Candidate in Communication Sciences and Digital Culture** at the University of Urbino Carlo Bo (Italy). With a background in linguistics and communication studies, she studies how digital platforms and algorithmic amplification shape political communication, and how these dynamics unfold across borders.

Her research combines computational methods with close attention to language and social context, working mainly across Brazilian and Italian cases. Her work has been published in international journals, presented at international conferences, and developed through research stays at the DMRC (Queensland University of Technology, Australia) and ZeMKI (University of Bremen, Germany).

She now serves as a Teaching Assistant at Urbino for Internet Studies and for AI and Communication, at both undergraduate and graduate level. She has previously taught university courses in Portuguese and Italian, in both Italy and Brazil, and worked as a journalist and web writer for regional media in Italy.

Her path into research began as an undergraduate at the University of São Paulo, where she conducted a research project in applied linguistics, on multilingualism and additional languages. She also holds a teacher-training degree focused on active learning methodologies.
